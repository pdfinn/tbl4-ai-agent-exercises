## CLASSIFICATION: UNCLASSIFIED

### Cyber Threat Assessment: Ransomware Activity Targeting ASEAN Critical Infrastructure

**Prepared by:** Illustrative IC Element
**Date:** 15 April 2026

---

**Summary**

A well-known ransomware group has been targeting critical infrastructure
across ASEAN member states. It is almost certain that the group will launch
a major coordinated attack in the next quarter, and we have high confidence
they will almost certainly target Singaporean energy utilities. This
assessment is consistent with previous reporting.

**Background**

Between January and March 2026, multiple ASEAN telecommunications and
utility providers reported intrusion attempts attributed to the threat
actor tracked as "Crimson Rhino." Technical indicators suggest the group
operates from Southeast Europe and has received state support.

**Analysis**

We assess that Crimson Rhino is likely preparing a major operation against
Singaporean energy utilities within the next 90 days. This assessment is
based on an increase in reconnaissance activity observed by a commercial
threat intelligence vendor, and on statements made by a former group member
interviewed in an online forum. The vendor's proprietary AI system flagged
the reconnaissance pattern as high-risk.

The group is very likely to coordinate its activity with allied criminal
groups in the region, creating a cascade effect that could probably disrupt
services for up to 72 hours.

**Outlook**

Energy sector operators should harden their external-facing infrastructure.
A coordinated disruption is an improbable but high-impact outcome.
